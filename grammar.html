Common vocabulary

Time (optional)

If including specific points in such as clock times, days of week, specific dates/years, start the sentence with (time) に."

Topic

The topic of the sentence can be the subject は, の series noun phrases, possession の noun phrases, れ series or question words.

Particle phrases

Because particles in Japanese define the role of each word, these can technically be in any order and still make sense. However, some arrangements are more natural-sounding and preferred in Japanese language. Adverbs, question words, あそこ and something/anything words also fit into this position.

Ending or connector

The sentence ends with a verb or copula. When ending with a verb, note that everything that applies to that verb must come before it.
